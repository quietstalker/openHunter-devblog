Until today the reserve was quiet between your own footsteps. Now the forest sounds alive.

![Forest]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/forest-sound.jpg)

## A sound for every place

Hirschfelden is split into areas: forest, open fields, wet meadows, clear-felled patches. As in the original, each of them has its own background. In the forest the wind moves through the crowns, insects and crickets buzz, a tree creaks somewhere, a branch falls, a crow calls in the distance. Walk out onto a field and the sound changes with the ground under your feet.

## Never the same twice

The game does not play one long recording. It mixes many short sounds and repeats them at random intervals, and some layers take turns over time. openHunter now follows the same rules, so the forest does not loop in an obvious way. Wind, rain and the time of day change how loud each layer is.

## From your own game files

Most of these sounds are stored in a format we could not read before. openHunter now decodes them directly from the sound banks of your installed theHunter Classic. Nothing from the game is included in openHunter itself.
