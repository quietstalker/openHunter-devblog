For the first time, time passes in the reserve: morning, midday and evening.

![Morning at the lodge]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/lodge.jpg)

## Time of day

The hunt has its own clock now. It starts at nine in the morning, as in the original, and runs twice as fast as real time. Just like the game, there is no night: at seven in the evening the day ends and it is five in the morning of the next day. The HunterMate clock shows this time.

## The sun

The sun follows the same path as in the original: it rises in the east, stands in the south at noon and sets in the west. Light on the ground, trees and animals changes with it, warm white during the day and orange in the morning and evening.

## Sky

Hirschfelden has the real sky from the original files now, sun included. It changes through the day, blue at noon and pink and orange around the sun at sunset.

![Sky]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/sky.jpg)

## Fog

Distant hills fade into haze, and its colour depends on whether you face the sun or look away from it. Towards evening the haze thickens and settles in the valleys.

![Valley haze]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/valley.jpg)

## Grass

Grass grows underfoot. It follows the original's rules: a different kind on each type of ground, one on fields, another in the forest, another on slopes. Around the hunter it is always thick, and it thins out smoothly in the distance.

![Field]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/field.jpg)

## Shadows

The sun casts shadows from trunks, houses and hills. They are soft at the edges and work like the original's. Cloud shadows drift across the ground.

![Forest shadows]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/forest.jpg)

## A picture like the game's

We added the original's frame processing: glow, saturation, and brightness that adapts as if your eyes were getting used to the light. The sky is the same deep blue as in the game and gets lighter towards the horizon. We checked it against screenshots from the real game.

## Weather and wind

Wind comes in gusts and changes direction like in the original, and animals smell you along it. On overcast days the fog gets thicker and the sun dimmer.

## Sound

The first sounds from the game: the hunter's footsteps on different ground and deer calling in the distance. The farther the animal, the quieter the call, and you can hear which side it comes from.

## Clouds

Cirrus clouds are in the sky, as in the original: thin streaks high above the ground, slowly drifting with the wind.

## Trees

Leaves and bark are lit the way the original lights them. The inside of a crown is darker, leaves glow when you look at them against the sun, and branches catch highlights.

## Ground

The ground has relief and highlights now. We fixed a bug that made ordinary slopes look like bare rock, so the slope behind the Trophy Lodge is green like in the game.

## Houses, animals and the rifle

Models have depth now: the cabin logs have relief, you can see the muscles on a deer, and the rifle's metal shines in the sun.

## Water

The lakes and rivers of Hirschfelden have water instead of dry beds. No reflections yet; they come next.

![Lake]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/lake.jpg)
![Bridge]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/bridge.jpg)

## Evening

Evening fog arrives at the same time as in the original: by six the valleys fill with haze.

![Dusk]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-03/dusk.jpg)
