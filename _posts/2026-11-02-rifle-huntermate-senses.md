A big day. Here is what landed in the engine.

## HunterMate in your hand

You now hold the HunterMate in your left hand, first person, the same way as in the original. The screen shows a live map of the reserve, with distance walked and the clock along the bottom. The glass on top carries the HUNTERMATE and OUTDOOR PROOF print like the real device.

![HunterMate]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-02/huntermate.jpg)

## Tracks and identifying animals

We went through the HunterMate screens next to screenshots from the original and fixed how the animal's weight is shown when you read a track. It now rounds the same way the game does.

![Following a trail]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-02/huntermate-trail.jpg)

## Trophy score

We worked out how the game scores each species. Deer, moose, sheep, goats, bison and boar each have their own formula based on antler or horn measurements. The score and the reward already match the original. Next up is the antler generator, so every animal carries its own measurements.

## Coats

Every animal gets its coat by the same rule the original uses. Roe deer have four: common, albino, melanistic and piebald. The rare ones are exactly as rare as in the game, so an albino in the woods is real luck.

![Roe deer coats]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-02/coats.jpg)

## Stand, crouch, go prone

Crouch, stand up, walk or run. Movement and eye height come from the original's animations, and animals can tell whether you are standing, crouching or lying down.

## Walk, trot, run

Animals walk, trot and run like they do in the game. A mildly spooked animal trots off and leaves trot prints; a badly spooked one bolts.

## The rifle

The starter .243 with its 4x32 scope is in your hands. You can raise it, fire and reload: the bolt works and the round goes from your fingers into the chamber. The scope has the original reticle, it sways, and holding your breath steadies it, just like the game. Zoom and the time it takes to aim come from the original. The magazine holds one round, like the real starter rifle.

## Animals that never shipped

The developer menu now lists characters that never made it into the game: a wolverine, a mandarin duck, a wendigo and a few more. You cannot hunt them, since they have no behaviour or health data, but you can look at them in the world.

![Cut animals]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2026-11-02/cut-animals.jpg)

## Animal senses

Animals now see, hear and smell the way they do in the original. Running is heard from far away, walking from closer, and standing still makes no noise at all. While you move or turn your head fast, an animal spots you from wider angles. Scent follows the wind, so come in from downwind. After a shot, animals flinch half a second later, and a spooked animal stays alert for a long time.

## Trees and buildings are solid

Objects and trees now have collision from the original files. A bullet stops in a trunk if a tree is in the way. Animals cannot see through the forest, so sneaking up behind trees works. You can no longer walk through walls or trunks.
